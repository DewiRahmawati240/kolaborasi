How Used CDs Are Transformed into a Futuristic Desk Lamp

The Process of Recycling Used Compact Discs

Paragraph 1

General Statement - Adinda Lutfi Assalwa

Lihat detail

Electronic waste is one of the environmental problems caused by the rapid development of technology. Unused compact discs (CDs) are often discarded when they are damaged or no longer needed. However, these CDs contain polycarbonate materials that can still be reused. One creative way to manage this waste is by transforming old CDs into a futuristic desk lamp called "Lumiorb".

The transformation begins when eight unused CDs are cleaned to remove dust and dirt. The CDs are then carefully cut using scissors into small geometric mosaic pieces, such as squares and triangles.

Paragraph 2

The Formation of a New Product - Dewi Rahmawati

Lihat detail

The cut CD pieces are combined with a balloon mold and a cardboard stand to create a new object. Through the process of arranging and attaching using hot glue, the individual CD pieces form a spherical decorative layer around the mold, leaving small gaps for illumination.

Paragraph 3

The Result and Its Impact - Evena Umahatul Latifah

Lihat detail

As a result, old compact discs are transformed into a desk lamp that has both functional and aesthetic value. This transformation reduces the amount of electronic waste and encourages the reuse of available materials.

Conclusion

Recycling used CDs into a futuristic desk lamp is a creative way to reduce electronic waste. It shows that unused materials can be transformed into useful and attractive products.
